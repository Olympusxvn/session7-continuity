# Ch.2 — Fake tide, public death, backup wallet (Day 3 twist)

Booth 7 still dripped. Minh still had the left cup on. The Tide Ledger was open to last night’s clean page: Red Buoy, NGH/USDC, wait sixty, stay flat. Alive. Independent. A baby whale who had done the rule right.

Lan’s cracked tablet lit again. Same cluster. Same name. “Second pulse,” she said. “You waited. Now you earn.”

He waited the hour. The tide did not invert from his greed. He entered.

The book ate him in public. Harbor Node’s wall showed the liquidation like a funeral notice: Minh’s main wallet — grey-hoodie money, thin capital — zeroed on NGH/USDC. Traders laughed the way the floor laughs when a regular dies on-screen. Oracle did not blink.

After, in the alley under the bridge joint, he saw the rest: the Red Buoy wallets were a script. Lan had seeded the fake Tide Signal for a rival book — North Pier Capital — and used his wait as cover. Mentor. Mentee. Done.

He opened the Tide Ledger’s last page. The backup seed, locked since Ch.0, left the paper. A second wallet woke. Minh was dead on the order book and alive in the backpack. Allegiance flipped: no more Oracle reads. No more independent smile at Booth 7. The baby whale had a new side — against the woman who taught him the tide.
